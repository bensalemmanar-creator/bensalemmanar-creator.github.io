About me

From business questions
to clear answers.

I studied Business Administration with a minor in Business Analytics, graduating on the honour roll with a 3.64/4 in my final year. I’m now in the Master’s in Business Analytics programme at South Mediterranean University (M1 GPA 3.82/4) and following DataCamp’s Associate Data Scientist track, where I earned the SQL Associate certification.

What I enjoy most is the step from a messy dataset to a recommendation someone can act on: which forecasting model to trust, which customers to re-engage, which variables really move a price. Most of my projects end with a “so what” for the business, not just a model score.

Before the Master’s I worked in operations and marketing — handling more than 50 import/export shipments at a logistics company in Addis Ababa, organising internship data at a university career centre, and running social-media campaigns. That taught me how decisions actually get made, and why the people using an analysis matter as much as the analysis itself. It’s also why I design: in Figma, by hand and with AI tools, so insights land in interfaces people can use.
